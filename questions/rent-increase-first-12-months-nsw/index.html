Can rent go up in the first 12 months or when I renew my lease in NSW?

Short answer

No. Rent can’t be increased in the first 12 months of a tenancy, and after an increase the landlord must wait at least 12 months before the next one. Renewing your lease doesn’t reset the clock.

When a renewal counts as the same tenancy

A new or renewed agreement counts as the same tenancy for rent increase purposes if:

  • the landlord hasn’t changed
  • at least one tenant on the agreement is the same
  • the tenant hasn’t moved out between agreements

Which agreements the 12-month rule covers

  • periodic (continuing) agreements
  • fixed-term agreements of 2 years or more
  • fixed-term agreements under 2 years that began on or after 13 December 2024

In NSW the rule follows your tenancy, not the property. That’s different from Queensland, where an increase for a previous tenant also counts.

Social housing

In social housing, rent can change more than once a year when it’s only because of a change to your rent rebate.
