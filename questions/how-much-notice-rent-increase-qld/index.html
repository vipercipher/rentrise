How much notice is needed for a rent increase in Queensland?

Short answer

At least 2 months’ written notice for a house, unit or townhouse, and at least 4 weeks’ written notice for a room in a rooming house. The new rent also can’t start until 12 months after the current rent began.

What the notice must include

The notice should be in writing and state:

  • the new rent amount
  • the day the increase takes effect
  • the date the rent was last increased for the property

Notice periods at a glance

SituationMinimum notice
House, unit or townhouse (general tenancy)2 months, in writing
Room in a rooming or boarding house4 weeks, in writing
Signing a new agreement at a higher rentNo separate notice, but it must still be 12 months since the last increase

Example

You receive written notice on 20 September 2026 that the rent will go up on 10 November 2026. Two months from 20 September is 20 November, so the notice is too short. The earliest the new rent can start is 20 November 2026, and only if the 12-month rule is also met.

A verbal notice, such as a phone call or a comment during an inspection, isn’t enough.
