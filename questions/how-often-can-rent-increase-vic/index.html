How often can rent be increased in Victoria?

Short answer

Once every 12 months for most agreements. Periodic agreements that started before 19 June 2019 can be increased once every 6 months.

How often, by agreement type

Your agreementHow often rent can increase
Periodic, started on or after 19 June 2019Once every 12 months
Periodic, started before 19 June 2019Once every 6 months
Fixed termOnly with a rent increase clause, and no more than once every 12 months

How the 12 months is counted

The new rent can’t start less than 12 months after your last rent increase took effect. Signing a new agreement doesn’t allow an earlier increase.

Example

Your rent last went up on 1 October 2025. The earliest the next increase can start is 1 October 2026, and you still need 90 days’ notice on the official form.
